# Japanese Vocabulary

| Word | Hiragana | Meaning in English | Type of Word | Example Used in a Sentence | Example English Translation | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 語彙 | ごい | vocabulary | noun | 語彙を増やしたいです。 | I want to increase my vocabulary. | Common academic/study word |
| 楽しみ | たのしみ | looking forward to; enjoyment | noun / な-adjective | 日本に行くのが楽しみです。 | I'm looking forward to going to Japan. | Often used with 「〜が楽しみです」 |
| 気軽に | きがるに | casually; without hesitation | adverb | 気軽に質問してください。 | Please feel free to ask questions. | Comes from 気軽 (easygoing/casual) |
| 片割れ | かたわれ | one half of a pair; other half | noun | 靴の片割れをなくした。 | I lost one of my shoes. | literary/poetic; "other half" |
| 実際 | じっさい | in reality; actually | adverb / noun | 実際にやってみると難しい。 | When you actually try it, it's difficult. | contrasts expectation vs reality |
| 基本 | きほん | basics; foundation; fundamental | noun / な-adjective | 日本語の基本を勉強しています。 | I am studying the basics of Japanese. | Often used in compounds like 基本的 (basic/fundamental) |
| 耐える | たえる | to endure; to withstand | ichidan verb | 痛みに耐えなければならない。 | I must endure the pain. | Often used for pain, stress, hardship, pressure, etc. |
| そっくり | そっくり | exactly alike; identical; entirely | adverb / な-adjective | あの二人は顔がそっくりです。 | Those two look exactly alike. | Can also mean "entirely" as in お金をそっくり盗まれた。 |
| 起きる | おきる | to wake up; to occur; to happen | ichidan verb | 毎朝七時に起きます。 | I wake up at 7 every morning. | Has multiple meanings depending on context. |
| 起こる | おこる | to occur; to happen | る-verb | 事故が突然起こった。 | An accident suddenly occurred. | Usually used for events/incidents happening. Compare with 起きる. |
| 配信 | はいしん | livestream; streaming; broadcast distribution | noun / する-verb | 彼は毎日ゲーム配信をしている。 | He streams games every day. | Commonly used for online streaming and livestreams. |
| 相手 | あいて | opponent; partner; other person | noun | 相手が強すぎた。 | The opponent was too strong. | Meaning changes depending on context. |
| 確認 | かくにん | confirmation; checking | noun / する-verb | メールを確認してください。 | Please check the email. | Very common in business and daily conversation. |
| テーマ | テーマ | theme; topic; subject | noun | 今日のテーマは日本文化です。 | Today's theme is Japanese culture. | Loanword from English "theme." |
| 具体的 | ぐたいてき | concrete; specific | な-adjective | もっと具体的に説明してください。 | Please explain more specifically. | Often contrasted with 抽象的. |
| 例 | れい | example; instance | noun | 例を挙げて説明してください。 | Please explain with examples. | Used in explanations. |
| 複雑 | ふくざつ | complex; complicated | な-adjective | この問題はとても複雑です。 | This problem is very complex. | Often used for systems, situations, emotions, etc. |
| 嘘つき | うそつき | liar | noun | あいつは嘘つきだ。 | That guy is a liar. | Can sound harsh depending on tone. |
| 具合 | ぐあい | condition; state; how something is going | noun | 今日は体の具合が悪い。 | I'm not feeling well today. | Often used for health or condition. |
| 深い | ふかい | deep | い-adjective | この話はとても深い。 | This story is very deep. | Physical or abstract depth. |
| 役に立つ | やくにたつ | to be useful; to be helpful | compound verb | この情報はとても役に立つ。 | This information is very useful. | Common expression. |
| 教室 | きょうしつ | classroom | noun | 教室で日本語を勉強しています。 | I am studying Japanese in the classroom. | Also used for courses. |
| 浴衣 | ゆかた | yukata; light cotton summer kimono | noun | 夏祭りで浴衣を着ました。 | I wore a yukata at the summer festival. | Casual traditional garment. |
| 観察 | かんさつ | observation; to observe | noun / する-verb | 動物の行動を観察した。 | I observed animal behavior. | Careful observation. |
| 種類 | しゅるい | variety; kind; type; category | noun | 動物園にはたくさんの種類の動物がいる。 | There are many kinds of animals at the zoo. | Common classification word. |
| 翻訳 | ほんやく | translation | noun / する-verb | 彼女はそれを一語一語翻訳した。 | She translated it word by word. | Used for translating text. |
| 文型 | ぶんけい | sentence pattern | noun | この文型を使って文を書いてください。 | Please write sentences using this sentence pattern. | Common in language learning. |
| 助詞 | じょし | particle | noun | 助詞の使い方を間違えました。 | I made a mistake in particle usage. | Key grammar term. |
| 副詞 | ふくし | adverb | noun | この副詞は動詞と一緒に使います。 | This adverb is used with verbs. | Grammar term. |
| 接続詞 | せつぞくし | conjunction | noun | 接続詞を使って二つの文を繋げてください。 | Connect the two sentences using a conjunction. | Grammar term. |
| 数詞 | すうし | numeral; quantifier | noun | 日本語には特別な数詞があります。 | Japanese has special numerals. | Grammar term. |
| 助数詞 | じょすうし | counter | noun | 助数詞を正しく使うのは難しいです。 | It is difficult to use counters correctly. | Japanese counting words. |
| 疑問詞 | ぎもんし | interrogative word | noun | 「どこ」「なに」「いつ」などは疑問詞です。 | "Where", "what", "when", etc. are interrogative words. | Question words. |
| 目次 | もくじ | table of contents | noun | 本の目次を見て章を探した。 | I looked at the table of contents to find the chapter. | Used in books. |
| 索引 | さくいん | index | noun | この単語を索引で調べてください。 | Please look up this word in the index. | Used in books and references. |
| 発音 | はつおん | pronunciation | noun / する-verb | 日本語の発音は英語と違います。 | Japanese pronunciation is different from English. | Language-learning term. |
| 主語 | しゅご | subject | noun | この文の主語は何ですか。 | What is the subject of this sentence? | Grammar term. |
| 述語 | じゅつご | predicate | noun | 日本語では述語が文の最後に来ます。 | In Japanese, the predicate comes at the end of the sentence. | Grammar term. |
| 目的語 | もくてきご | object | noun | 目的語は主語の後に置きます。 | The object is placed after the subject. | Grammar term. |
| 主題 | しゅだい | topic | noun | この文章の主題は何ですか。 | What is the topic of this passage? | Writing/grammar term. |
| 丁寧 | ていねい | polite; courteous | noun / な-adjective | 彼はいつも丁寧に話します。 | He always speaks politely. | Very common. |
| 普通 | ふつう | normal; ordinary | noun / な-adjective | 彼の考え方は普通と違います。 | His way of thinking is different from normal. | Common everyday word. |
| 例外 | れいがい | exception | noun | この規則には例外がありません。 | There are no exceptions to this rule. | Opposite of rule. |
| 事実 | じじつ | truth; fact | noun | 事実を隠すことはできない。 | You cannot hide the truth. | Common in formal speech. |
| 定める | さだめる | to decide; to establish; to set | ichidan verb | 規則を定める必要がある。 | It is necessary to establish rules. | formal/legal tone |
| 並べる | ならべる | to line up; to arrange | ichidan verb | 本をきれいに並べる。 | Arrange the books neatly. | physical or abstract ordering |
| 真 | しん | truth; reality; true | noun | 真を求める。 | Seek the truth. | often in compounds (真実 etc.) |
| 沈む | しずむ | to sink; to set (sun) | う-verb | 太陽が海に沈む。 | The sun sinks into the sea. | also emotional “to feel down” |
| 掴む | つかむ | to grab; to seize | う-verb | チャンスを掴む。 | Seize the opportunity. | physical or abstract grasping |
| 映る | うつる | to be reflected; to be projected | う-verb | 鏡に顔が映る。 | My face is reflected in the mirror. | passive-like meaning |
| 旅路 | たびじ | journey; travel route | noun | 長い旅路の途中です。 | I am in the middle of a long journey. | literary nuance |
| 振り返る | ふりかえる | to look back; to reflect | う-verb | 過去を振り返る。 | Look back on the past. | physical + mental reflection |
| 瞬間 | しゅんかん | moment; instant | noun | その瞬間が大切だ。 | That moment is important. | very short time |
| 迎える | むかえる | to welcome; to reach (a time) | ichidan verb | 新しい年を迎える。 | To welcome the new year. | also “to reach (a time)” |
| 隠す | かくす | to hide; to conceal | う-verb | 真実を隠す。 | Hide the truth. | physical or abstract hiding |
| 偶然 | ぐうぜん | coincidence; accidental | noun / な-adjective | 偶然会った。 | I met them by chance. | not planned |
| 頃 | ころ | around (time); when | noun/suffix | 子供の頃 | When I was a child. | time approximation |
| 浮かべる | うかべる | to float; to bring to mind | ichidan verb | 笑顔を浮かべる。 | Put on a smile. | physical + mental image |
| 賭ける | かける | to bet; to gamble | ichidan verb | すべてを賭ける。 | Bet everything. | risk sense |
| 我慢 | がまん | patience; endurance | noun / する-verb | 我慢できない。 | I can't stand it. | restraint/endurance |
| 対策 | たいさく | measure; countermeasure | noun | 感染対策をする。 | Take infection countermeasures. | solution/strategy |
| お気に入り | おきにいり | favorite | noun | この店がお気に入りです。 | This shop is my favorite. | common daily word |
| 単純 | たんじゅん | simple; naive | な-adjective | 問題は単純だ。 | The problem is simple. | simple vs naive nuance |
| 仕組み | しくみ | mechanism; structure | noun | この仕組みは複雑だ。 | This mechanism is complex. | how something works |
| 効果 | こうか | effect; result | noun | 薬の効果がある。 | The medicine is effective. | outcome/impact |
| 厚意 | こうい | kindness; goodwill | noun | ご厚意に感謝します。 | Thank you for your kindness. | formal polite word |
| 占う | うらなう | to predict; to fortune-tell | う-verb | 未来を占う。 | Predict the future. | fortune telling |
| まとも | まとも | decent; normal; serious | な-adjective | まともな仕事 | A decent job. | standard/normal state |
| 言語 | げんご | language | noun | 言語を学ぶのは楽しいです。 | Learning languages is fun. | Common academic word |
| 学ぶ | まなぶ | to learn; to study | ichidan verb | 日本語を学ぶ。 | I study Japanese. | More formal than 勉強する |
| 追加 | ついか | addition; to add | noun / する-verb | 単語を追加しました。 | I added the word. | Common in tech/business contexts |
| 予定 | よてい | plan; schedule | noun | 明日の予定は何ですか。 | What are your plans for tomorrow? | Commonly used for plans and appointments. |
| 行われる | おこなわれる | to be held; to take place; to be conducted | verb (passive) | コンサートは来週行われます。 | The concert will be held next week. | Passive form of 行う; used for events, meetings, ceremonies |
| 軽く | かるく | lightly; slightly; casually | adverb | 軽く運動しました。 | I did some light exercise. | Common adverb |
| 興奮 | こうふん | excitement; arousal | noun / する-verb | 試合に興奮した。 | I got excited during the match. | Also used as 興奮する (to get excited). |
| 情報 | じょうほう | information | noun | インターネットで情報を集めます。 | I gather information on the internet. | Very common; used for facts, data, and useful knowledge. |
| 手段 | しゅだん | means; method; way | noun | インターネットは情報を集める手段の一つです。 | The internet is one means of gathering information. | Refers to a method or means used to achieve a goal. |
| 後悔 | こうかい | regret | noun / する-verb | もっと勉強すればよかったと後悔しています。 | I regret that I should have studied more. | Commonly used as 後悔する, "to regret." |
| 表す | あらわす | to express; to represent; to show | う-verb | この言葉は感謝の気持ちを表しています。 | This word expresses a feeling of gratitude. | Often used for expressing feelings, meanings, symbols, or ideas. |
| 否定 | ひてい | denial; negation; to deny | noun / する-verb | 彼はその話を否定しました。 | He denied that story. | Also used in grammar for negative forms, e.g. 否定形. |
| ほぼ | ほぼ | almost; nearly; approximately | adverb | 宿題はほぼ終わりました。 | The homework is almost finished. | Very common; indicates something is nearly complete or approximately true. |
