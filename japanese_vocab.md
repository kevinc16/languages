# Japanese Vocabulary

| Word | Hiragana | Meaning in English | Type of Word | Example Used in a Sentence | Example English Translation | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 語彙 | ごい | vocabulary | noun | 語彙を増やしたいです。 | I want to increase my vocabulary. | Common academic/study word |
| 楽しみ | たのしみ | looking forward to; enjoyment | noun / な-adjective | 日本に行くのが楽しみです。 | I'm looking forward to going to Japan. | Often used with 「〜が楽しみです」 |
| 気軽に | きがるに | casually; without hesitation | adverb | 気軽に質問してください。 | Please feel free to ask questions. | Comes from 気軽 (easygoing/casual) |
| 片割れ | かたわれ | one half of a pair; other half | noun | 靴の片割れをなくした。 | I lost one of my shoes. | literary/poetic; "other half" |
| 実際 | じっさい | in reality; actually | adverb / noun | 実際にやってみると難しい。 | When you actually try it, it's difficult. | contrasts expectation vs reality |
| 基本 | きほん | basics; foundation; fundamental | noun / な-adjective | 日本語の基本を勉強しています。 | I am studying the basics of Japanese. | Often used in compounds like 基本的 (basic/fundamental) |
| 耐える | たえる | to endure; to withstand | ichidan verb | 痛みに耐えなければならない。 | I must endure the pain. | Often used for pain, stress, hardship, pressure, etc. |
| ... |
| 追加 | ついか | addition; to add | noun / する-verb | 単語を追加しました。 | I added the word. | Common in tech/business contexts |
| 予定 | よてい | plan; schedule | noun | 明日の予定は何ですか。 | What are your plans for tomorrow? | Often used to talk about future plans or schedules |
